Use this to compute your current grade using the categories your instructor uses: Reading Logs, Essays, Absences, Final Portfolio.

Recommended steps:
1) Enter your earned points for each item below.
2) Sum points to get "Earned so far".
3) Divide by total possible to get your current percent.
