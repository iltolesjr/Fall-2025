1) Opening letter: longing + danger

- Anchor phrases: "I cannot say", "I won't write", "for safety"

4) Sea imagery → memory + mortality

## Putting them in conversation (optional comparative move)
- Witness vs Want: Letters preserve truth (Danticat) while objects rewrite identity (Cisneros).
- Material grammar: The sea (elemental) vs the doll (manufactured) both become containers for self.
- Dignity: Prayer/ritual language vs brag/boast cadence—different music, same fight for worth.
