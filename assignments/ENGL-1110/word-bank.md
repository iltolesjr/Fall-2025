# Word Bank — ENGL Reflections (Voice‑First, Past Tense)

Use this vocabulary to vary diction while keeping a clear, academic tone. Past tense forms are shown or implied.

## Connectors (cohesion)

- and, but, or, therefore, however, meanwhile, later, then, finally
- in addition, moreover, nonetheless, consequently, as a result

## Rhetorical verbs (analysis, past tense)

- argued, suggested, revealed, illustrated, emphasized, critiqued
- contrasted, connected, framed, situated, complicated, clarified

## Evidence moves (past tense)

- I introduced, I quoted, I summarized, I analyzed, I concluded
- I linked claims to evidence; I showed cause and effect; I traced a pattern

## Theme vocabulary

- separation, endurance, memory, identity, material culture, privilege
- policing, protest, family decisions, resilience, compassion

## Style cues (voice‑first)

- concise claims, precise nouns/verbs, avoid filler, avoid instruction language in final
- lead with lived experience, follow with textual support

## Tone markers

- reflective, direct, honest, measured, compassionate, critical when needed

## Page citation language (audit‑safe)

- “assigned pp. 3–29,” “chapter reference,” “PDF pp. 164–180,” “pages pending verification”

## Transitions (past tense framing)

- I began by…, I moved to…, I returned to…, I concluded by…
- Previously, I showed…, Later, I connected…, Finally, I underscored…
